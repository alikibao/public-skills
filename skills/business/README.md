# Business

Skills for putting AI to work inside a business.

## Model-invoked

Model- or user-reachable (rich trigger phrasing so the model can reach for them).

- **[mission](./mission/SKILL.md)**: Give your AI a mission. A grilling interview that starts from whatever problem you want AI to help with, works out what's really going on, and uncovers what your AI needs to know and do: the outcome, how the work really runs, the unwritten rules and judgement calls, and the limits. It ends with a brief to give your AI, or a plain "fix this first" when AI isn't the answer.
