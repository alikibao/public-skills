## What it does

`mission` interviews you about your business until your AI has what it needs to help with a real problem.

Most business AI waits to be asked, and when it is asked it gives the industry average. It's like a very smart new hire who knows nothing about your business and forgets everything after each conversation. Two things are missing: a clear job, and your business's own know-how. That means how the work really runs, the rules nobody wrote down and the judgement calls your best people make without thinking. Most of that has never been said out loud, because nobody had to.

The skill gets it out of your head. It uses the grilling method from [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), adapted for business owners:

- It asks in **rounds**, and every question comes with its recommended answer. Reply "yes" to accept, push back where it's wrong, or say "I don't know". Unknowns become open questions, never guesses.
- It **starts with your problem**, not with AI. It asks for a recent real example, finds the cause behind the symptom, and agrees with you what kind of help this is. That might be an ongoing job for AI to own, a piece of work to get done, AI that keeps giving generic answers, or a team that isn't using AI. Sometimes the problem isn't an AI problem yet, and it will say so.
- It **uncovers what you didn't think to say**. It asks for the last real example, chases words like "usually" and "it depends", and asks for the exceptions. It asks what your best person knows that a new hire wouldn't, and what would make you wince if the AI did it.

At the end you get a **brief for your AI**, shaped to your problem and written so you can paste it into a ChatGPT or Claude project or give it to an agent. Its heart is a list of your rules and judgement calls in the form "When X, do Y, because Z". You also get a short page for yourself: what the session uncovered, open questions, first actions with names and dates, and the next problems worth tackling.

## When to reach for it

Use it when you want AI to take something on in your business and aren't sure what to tell it. For example, when you:

- want AI to follow up quotes, answer customer messages, chase payments, prepare reports or keep an eye on something;
- keep getting generic answers, or keep re-explaining your business to AI;
- have paid for AI and your team isn't using it;
- want your AI to work towards a goal instead of waiting for questions;
- aren't sure whether AI is even the answer to the problem in front of you.

It works for any kind of business. It was written with South African owners in mind, so it knows about POPIA, rand and how much work happens on WhatsApp, but nothing in it depends on being in South Africa.

You don't need to install anything. Copy the [SKILL.md](../../skills/business/mission/SKILL.md) into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI chat, and it starts the session. In Claude Code, Codex and other agents that support skills, you can also install it with the rest of this repo, and it starts on its own when your request fits.

## It's working if

- You tell it something about your business that you've never written down anywhere.
- You push back on at least one of its recommendations. A session where you agree with everything mostly reflects its thinking, not yours.
- The brief contains rules only your business would have, not advice any business could use.
- Numbers you don't know stay marked as unknown, with a name next to who'll find them.
- It's willing to tell you to fix something else first.
- You'd be happy for a new member of staff to read the brief.

## Common questions

**Do I need a paid AI plan?**
No. It works in any AI chat. A paid plan helps if your sessions run long, and it lets you keep the finished brief in a project so your AI can use it every day.

**How long does it take?**
Usually 20 to 40 minutes, in four to seven rounds of questions. You can answer briefly; it asks follow-ups where it needs more.

**What if I don't know the answer?**
Say so. "I don't know" turns into an open question with a person and a date next to it, or a "measure this first" step. It never fills gaps with made-up numbers or industry averages.

**Should I paste client information into it?**
No. The session is about how your business works, not about individual customers. When your problem involves personal information, it flags POPIA and recommends what should and shouldn't go into your AI. That's practical guidance, not legal advice.

**Why does it ask so many questions?**
Because the valuable part is what you wouldn't think to mention. In simulated sessions with five made-up businesses, each owner had four pieces of know-how they'd only share if asked the right question. The skill uncovered all 20; a plain AI chat uncovered 8. Sessions took about 30% longer.

**Is this Matt Pocock's grill-me?**
It uses the same interview method, the rounds, the recommended answers, and the split between facts and decisions, rewritten for business owners and pointed at what an AI needs to know about your business. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Matt Pocock.

## Where it fits

Start here when you want AI to take on a job, a process or a problem in your business. Run it again about a month later, when you have real experience to learn from, and work through the next problems it parked along the way.
